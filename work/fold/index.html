The mess

A space made for daylight.

Warm peach surfaces, soft blue seating and citrus-green details keep the cafe optimistic, never childish, and leave room for sunlight and long pauses.

The marker work

Everyday objects, made special.

Coffee bags, takeaway cups and pastry boxes were designed as small folded objects: practical, tactile and recognisable from the first touch.

The payoff

A brand that keeps opening.

The website, posters and outdoor moments extend the cafe beyond its walls. Fold feels as inviting on a screen or a street corner as in a citrus garden.
